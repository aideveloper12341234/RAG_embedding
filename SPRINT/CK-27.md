# CK-27 Search by meaning as well as by exact words

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every piece of every document is prepared for search by meaning, and new or changed pieces are prepared in the daily job.
- Search by meaning and keyword search run together and their results are combined.
- Exact matches on equipment codes are not lost.
- Results are reranked by relevance to the alarm before the best passages are picked, and the match indication shown per document in CK-21 is derived from that ranking.
- The alarm set from CK-25 is re-run, and both the change against the baseline and the effect on response time are recorded.
